Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026

At FitPart, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimalism, both in design and data collection. FitPart is a tool to help you find laptop parts without requiring you to create an account.

  • Non-Personal Data: We may collect non-personal data such as browser type, operating system, and pages visited to help us optimize the website's performance and user experience.
  • Form Submissions: If you use our Google Form to request a laptop series, the information provided there is subject to Google's privacy policies. We only use this information to update our database and improve our service.

2. How We Use Your Information

The minimal tracking data we collect is used solely to:

  • Improve our website's functionality and content.
  • Analyze user trends to understand which laptop brands and series are most searched for.

3. Third-Party Links and Marketplaces

FitPart contains links to third-party marketplaces (e.g., Tokopedia, Shopee, Amazon). We do not share your data with these marketplaces. However, when you click on these links and leave our site, our Privacy Policy no longer applies. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any external sites you visit.

4. Cookies

We may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. A cookie is a small text file stored on your device that helps us remember your preferences. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though doing so may affect the functionality of our website.

5. Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your information. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". Your continued use of the website after any changes indicates your acceptance of the new policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our Support Section.
